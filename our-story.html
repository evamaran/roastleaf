Our Story

Roast & Leaf began as a tiny kitchen project — a couple of friends experimenting with beans, leaves, and brewing methods late into the night. What started as curiosity grew into a shared obsession with flavor, aroma, and the craft behind every cup.

Over time, we connected with small farms, independent roasters, and tea artisans who shared our values. Their stories shaped ours, and today we continue to build a community rooted in authenticity and appreciation for the craft.

We're still learning, still tasting, still refining — and we're grateful you're part of the journey.
